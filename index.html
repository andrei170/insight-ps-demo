Driveways

Four surfaces, and an honest answer about which one suits you

They are not interchangeable. The right one depends on the slope, the drainage, how many cars and what you want it to look like in ten years.

Block paving

The most repairable surface there is — lift a few blocks, fix what is underneath, put them back. Good for heavy use and awkward shapes.

Resin bound

Seamless, permeable and smooth underfoot. Needs a sound base beneath it, which is where most poor resin jobs go wrong.

Tarmac

Fast to lay, hard wearing and the best value per square metre on a long or wide drive.

Indian sandstone patios

Natural stone, laid on a full mortar bed with the falls set so water leaves rather than sits.

Paving repairs & maintenance

Sinking, weeds, loose blocks and failed edgings. Usually a repair, not a replacement — and we will say so.

Groundworks & site preparation

Dig-outs, sub-bases, drainage and levels. The part nobody sees and the part that decides everything.

Straight answers

What homeowners ask us

Which driveway material is best for my property?

It depends on the slope, the drainage and how the drive is used. Block paving is the most repairable, resin is the smoothest and most permeable, tarmac is the best value over a large area, and sandstone is for patios rather than parking. We will tell you which suits your ground rather than which we would rather lay.

How long will the work take?

A typical domestic driveway is a matter of days rather than weeks, but it depends on the dig-out, the drainage and the weather. We give you a realistic window at estimate stage, not an optimistic one.

Why does the sub-base matter so much?

Because nearly every failed driveway failed underneath, not on top. Sinking, rutting and standing water are almost always a base or drainage problem showing through the surface. It is the part of the job you never see and the part worth paying for.

Are you insured, and are you checked?

Yes, fully insured, and we are Checkatrade members. If you want to see the paperwork before we start, ask — that is a reasonable thing to want.

Will you tidy up afterwards?

Yes. The site goes back cleaner than we found it, and waste goes with us rather than sitting on your verge waiting for a skip.

Do you really do roofing as well as driveways?

Yes. Our skills extend from the ground to the roof, and on a lot of properties both need attention at the same time. One contractor, one point of contact.
